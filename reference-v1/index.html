How a project rises

From bare land to handover

Placeholder Pre-rendered construction time-lapse — bare land to finished structure. Scroll-scrub activates automatically when the all-intra encoded file is added.
  1. Phase 01 · 0–25%

    Ground first

    Subsurface investigation, instrumentation, setting out, and excavation. We read the ground before we ask anything of it.

  2. Phase 02 · 25–50%

    Foundations & structure

    Foundation works, waterproofing at the source, and the structure rising on ground we understand.

  3. Phase 03 · 50–75%

    Water & systems

    Hydraulic works, piping, pumps, electromechanical and sanitary systems — the building learns to move water.

  4. Phase 04 · 75–100%

    Commissioning & handover

    Testing, calibration, operator training — and the after-sales program begins where most suppliers end.
